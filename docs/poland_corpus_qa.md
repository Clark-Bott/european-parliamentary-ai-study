# Poland corpus QA — local build through 2026-09-25

The official Sejm per-statement HTML API produced **97,513 interventions / 32,231,985 words** for 2018–2026 through 25 September 2026. The normalized file is Git-ignored; its SHA-256 and size are in [the Polish corpus manifest](../data/manifests/poland_current_manifest.json). The full-file disk-backed audit passed: no duplicate speech IDs, empty speeches, or schema errors; it counted 34 repeated text hashes and flagged 213 interventions above 10,000 words for later review. These flags do not establish parser errors or valid speech boundaries.

The [cached-index/body audit](../data/manifests/poland_coverage_audit.json) found 480 target sitting days and 97,513 expected spoken bodies, with none absent from disk. A read-only comparison on 25 September of the live proceedings indexes for terms 8, 9 and 10 against their cached counterparts found the same 85, 196 and 199 target days. This checks index-day presence, not the live state of every statement body or transcript edits after retrieval. The [Polish gap report](../data/manifests/poland_unavailable_statements.json) is empty.

## Source provenance limitation

The tracked `source_manifest.jsonl` was used locally but its live acquisition additions were **not committed**. All 95,521 unique Polish files with manifest entries matched their recorded sizes and SHA-256 hashes. The older acquisition includes both HTTP 200 and HTTP 206 entries; neither code alone indicates a corrupt cached file. Another **2,458 cached statement bodies across 18 sitting days**, plus **16 day-metadata files**, have no corresponding source-manifest entry. Their bytes exist and were parsed, but their retrieval status and time cannot be reconstructed from the manifest. Do not label the corpus fully provenance-reconciled. Preserve the raw cache and investigate or re-fetch the missing entries with real retrieval metadata before claiming complete file-level provenance.

## Source-boundary screen

Ten interventions, selected by a deterministic full-file per-year SHA-256 rank (seed 2026), were checked against their cached official Sejm HTML and statement metadata. **Nine text matches were exact after whitespace/case folding; one was partial; all ten speaker names and dates matched the day metadata.** The partial is `sejm-term10-proceeding34-2025-05-07-statement175` (303 words). Its normalized `raw_text` occurs in the HTML, but the cleaned text removes stage-direction paragraphs; it also contains other speakers' inline interjections in this single official statement. This automated screen does not settle intervention boundaries or speaker attribution across the corpus. The detailed report is local at `results/reports/poland_source_boundary_cached.json` (Git-ignored); summary at [source-boundary verification](source_boundary_verification.md).
