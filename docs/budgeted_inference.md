# Budgeted inference and guarded corpus API diagnostic

## Parliamentary sample

After the six-country corpus passes integrity and coverage checks, use `--sample-budget USD` to limit estimated total Pangram 4 charges. This does **not** bypass full-corpus QA, 2018–2025 country/year coverage, or the required Spanish/Polish gap reports. The accepted German 2026 cutoff is informational, not a gate. `--max-cost USD` is an independent upper bound; when using both, `--sample-budget` must be no larger. Optional positive controls are budgeted **before** any parliamentary sample allocation. Sampled runs do not silently drop controls; if they cannot fit, the run stops.

The full corpus is divided into **nonempty country × calendar-month strata**, including pre-2022 controls and 2022 transition months. Only speeches with at least 40 source words are eligible. The planner gives each month one slot, then adds slots to the months with the smallest sampled share until the budget cannot reserve another slot. It charges each reserved slot at that month's *longest* eligible speech length, rounded up to 100 words. This is a conservative upper bound on the actual cost of the sample and can leave substantial funds unused, especially where a month contains one very long speech. A too-small budget stops before any paid request instead of dropping months or countries. The budget and cap are **local estimates**, not a provider-enforced spending limit; confirm Pangram's current billing rate and account balance.

For each stratum, the sampler selects a fixed number `n` of its `N` eligible interventions by a seeded SHA-256 priority of country, month and speech ID. Every eligible speech in that month has inclusion probability `n/N`, and selected records have `sampling_weight = N/n`. Fixed `n` is determined **before** ranking or querying any speeches and does not depend on the cost of the selected speeches or detector predictions. Word-weighted AI-only and AI-assisted shares use the ratio of weighted estimated AI/mixed words to weighted source words. Calendar months have one or more selected interventions; quarter/year/pre–post tables and length/role/party/term sensitivities use the same weights. Speaker-level reports are suppressed for samples. Cost and response tables refer to *selected* interventions, not the full population.

`results/<name>/reports/sampling_plan.json` stores the seed, source/sample SHA-256 checksums, price, reserved cost, sampled and eligible counts, inclusion probability by month, and control-file hash. `monthly_results.csv` includes `sampled_speeches` and the inverse-weighted speech estimate; the SVG title marks it as a sampled estimate. Separate monthly SVGs show AI-only, AI-assisted, and their combined share, for all countries and each country. On resume, a changed corpus/seed/budget/price/controls plan is rejected for an existing results directory. Paid responses still use the existing content/configuration cache; interrupted requests are not blindly resubmitted. Source and sample text, detector responses, and result tables remain Git-ignored.

**Statistical limit:** Equal-probability sampling within a month gives known design weights, but ratio shares are only estimates, not the exact full-corpus plots. The initial allocation optimizes geographic/time coverage rather than precision for specific parties, roles or long speeches. A single observation in a month is not a reliable prevalence estimate; no confidence intervals or design effects are computed. Do not make claims about individual speakers or overinterpret noisy plots. Review monthly sample sizes and detector calibration; raise the budget or narrow the explicitly documented scope if needed. Length-dependent nonresponse or unreviewed source gaps invalidate the interpretation.

## One-to-three-speech corpus API diagnostic

After a free `./run_experiment.sh --dry-run`, and once the combined corpus and required Spanish/Polish gap reports are ready, set `PANGRAM_API_KEY` in ignored `.env` and use `./run_experiment.sh --test-api --corpus data/processed/speeches.jsonl --max-cost 1 --confirm-paid-run`. This reads the **full combined corpus**, performs disk-backed QA and the same 2018–2025 country/year and Spanish/Polish gap checks as the paid study, then picks one real source-language speech from 2023 onward. The accepted German 2026 cutoff and any approval file are not gates. It selects distinct 40–100-word interventions by a reproducible hash of country, date and ID; the hash ranking does not use detector output or choose a purported AI example. `--test-country COUNTRY` optionally restricts selection; `--test-count N` supports up to three speeches. The `$1` limit is a local estimate, not a provider-enforced spend cap.

The initial model entitlement check is a read-only GET; each selected speech has at most one new Pangram POST, and previously completed/pending tasks use the same cache protections as the main pipeline. The default local ceiling is `$0.05` for one started 100-word block; a higher configured price fails before network access unless you explicitly change the cap. No corpus is built or downloaded. `results/api_test/selection.json` pins the corpus SHA-256, source metadata, model, cost, and text fingerprints before paid submission; retries with a changed selection fail rather than silently adding new tasks. `test_result.json` records each completed speech's date, ID, AI-only and AI-assisted share without its raw text. Both files and cached responses are Git-ignored. These very short speech labels can be unstable: the diagnostic establishes API integration on actual corpus text, **not** a representative prevalence estimate or proof that a speaker used AI. Check your actual account pricing and credits first; a software cap cannot guarantee the provider's final charge.
